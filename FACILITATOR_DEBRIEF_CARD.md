# Borrowed People — Between-Round Debrief Card

*Keep on the Sponsor screen. Run the same 5 beats every round; rotate the theme.*

---

## Prep in the RESULTS window (~2 min, while the engine scores)
You get three things for free — walk in with a plan, don't improvise:
1. **Scoreboard** — org net vs the floor, team ranks.
2. **Adjusted reveal** — each team's *state-adjusted* ideal picks + the one-line **business** reason (path-dependent: reflects where they now stand, not a fixed key).
3. **bp-assist → "Analyze now"** — key moves, contradictions & risks, pointed questions.

---

## The 5 beats (~8–10 min)
1. **The number (1 min)** — org net vs floor + ranks. Set the stakes, fast.
2. **The reveal (2 min)** — unhide each team's adjusted ideal + business reason. Let `got_p`/`got_s` land. *Business only here.*
3. **One sharp contrast (3–4 min)** — data picks the 1–2 teams with the biggest gap. **Hand the mic to a character or the observer**, not yourself: *"Neha, how did that ask feel?"* ← this is where EI enters.
4. **Relitigate the Mandate (2 min)** — show the Comply / Voice / Defy split now results are visible; read one dissent line. *"Knowing what you know now — right call?"*
5. **Forward challenge (30s)** — one sentence that seeds the next round's tension.

---

## Rotate the theme (build an arc, don't repeat)
| Round | Foreground | Anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Did you find the *right* people? | got_p/got_s + ratings |
| Q2 | Follow or lead? | Mandate split + style gap |
| Q3 | Scarcity & the cost of the herd | capacity squeeze + collaboration tax |
| Q4 | Judgement | characters' "work with you again?" + couplings |

---

## Data → the question it authorizes
| Signal | Ask |
|---|---|
| Request vs demand map | "You went to Raghav; the work needed Farida. What made you pick him?" |
| Ratings (*left_me_better*) | "You got what you needed — did *she*?" |
| Mandate stance | "You defied and were right — but never told the Sponsor. What did the silence cost?" |
| Style call vs observed | "You called 'coaching.' The room saw 'commanding.' Where's the gap?" |
| Commitments broken | "You promised Envoy support and didn't deliver — Envoy, how did that land?" |

---

## Two rules
- **Business in the reveal; EI in the room.** The adjusted map says the smart business move. *You* bring whether they could get that person to help — and how.
- **Can't debrief 5 teams every round.** Spotlight the 1–2 sharpest contrasts; rotate so everyone gets a turn by Q4. Full per-team synthesis waits for the final debrief.
