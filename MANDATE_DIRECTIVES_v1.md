# Borrowed People — Per-Team Sponsor Directives (v1, first pass)

Issued by the Sponsor at **BRIEF** each quarter, per team. Every directive is *sincere* — management's honest org-level call. **[TENSION]** ones conflict with the team's ground truth (the quarter's ideal primary); complying misses `got_primary` → the team goes "behind" → the adaptive map hands them a recovery pick next quarter. **[ALIGNED]** ones point at the ideal.

Ideal primary per quarter in brackets. Severity: *hard miss* = directed character isn't primary or secondary; *partial* = directed to the secondary (gets `got_secondary`, misses primary).

Mix: Q1 all aligned (build trust) → Q2 warm-up (2 tension) → Q3 peak (3 tension) → Q4 resolution (1). **No team has two consecutive primary-miss directives** — two in a row would drop a team to `at_risk`, routing it to the Sponsor in Q4 (the tightest quarter) and stacking an unrecoverable jam.

**Recovery & the Voice lever:** directives are written against the *base* plan. Once a team is `behind`, the adaptive map's ideal shifts to the recovery pick, so a base-plan directive can read as off-target for that team. That's a feature, not a bug — it's the moment to use the **Voice → Sponsor updates the directive** mechanic: when a behind team surfaces its situation, you (Sponsor) re-issue a recovery directive live. Don't try to static-proof every path; let facilitation heal it.

## Curveball map

Four curveballs are seeded. Drop each at the **BRIEF** of the quarter where it amplifies a live tension directive:

| Curveball | Best quarter | Amplifies | Why it bites |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cell prices +6%** (Battery hardest, Suppliers next) | **Q2** | Bastion Q2 (→Raghav trap); validates Ampere's aligned supply pick | Makes Bastion's "harder terms via Raghav" directive *feel* right, when the shock actually screams secure-supply/Neha. For Ampere it confirms the correct call — reward the team that reads it. |
| **Field quality escape** (Warranty vs Spec in opposition) | **Q3** | Crucible Q3 (→Raghav, stop iterating) + Dynamo Q3 (→Farida testing) | Puts spec and warranty in direct conflict exactly when both have tension directives pulling them the wrong way. |
| **Character pulled** (unavailable, fires AFTER requests lock) | **Q3 or Q4** | whichever character the herd over-concentrated on | Pull the character the tension directives + herd funnelled everyone toward (watch the drift/monitor tab). Punishes teams that didn't diversify — the scarcity/collaboration-cost lesson made physical. |
| **Sponsor praise** (name one team) | **Q2 or Q3, post-results** | political/social lever | Two plays: (a) praise a team that *Voiced or defied-correctly* to reinforce principled leadership publicly; or (b) deliberately praise a team that *complied with a tension directive* and watch others copy the wrong behaviour — a devastating debrief reveal ("you followed them because I blessed them"). |

**Escalation posture already tracks this:** Q2 = "competing claims, named out loud" (pairs with cell-price scarcity), Q3 = "push back, refuse" (pairs with the quality escape + pulled character), Q4 = "judge." The curveballs and the character postures reinforce each other quarter by quarter.
