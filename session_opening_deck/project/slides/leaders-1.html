Three leaders

Same organisation. Same objective. Same Monday.

Each of them needs the design lead's sign-off before anything on their programme can move. Each has one quarter. Here is what each one does on Monday morning.

The Commander

Sends the request up the chain with the Sponsor's name on it. Gets the meeting by Thursday. Says what he needs, and by when.

The Diplomat

Doesn't want to impose on a busy person. Mentions it at the stand-up. Hopes somebody volunteers. Keeps everyone comfortable.

The Borrower

Walks over. Asks what the design lead is actually worried about this quarter. Listens. Then asks for the sign-off — in those terms.
