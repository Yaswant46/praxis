Every quarter, three things from your team

01

The Request

One primary, one secondary. Who does your objective actually need this quarter — and who is the back-up if you don't get them?

02

The Style Call

Your lead declares the posture they will lead with — one of Goleman's six — and why. Your observer records what the room actually saw.

03

The Mandate

The Sponsor gives you a directive at every Brief. You decide: comply, voice your disagreement, or defy it — and you own what follows.

All three lock before the reveal. What you declared meets what you did, in the debrief.
