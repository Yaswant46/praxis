Elizabeth Holmes

Read it. Managed it.

Knew exactly what investors, the board and the press needed to believe.

Managed the belief, for years. The device never worked.

Theranos. The same skill — reading exactly what a room wants to hear — aimed at perception instead of reality. She read investors, a board of former statesmen, the press, and her own staff with real precision. She knew what each of them needed to believe, and she managed that belief: the demo that ran on a different machine, the lab results quietly re-run elsewhere, the employees who asked questions and were walked out. The device never worked. The reading was accurate for years; the thing being managed was what people saw, not what was true. Convicted of fraud in 2022. Keep it flat, keep it short. Land on: she read the room, and managed the impression the room had of her.
