The three artefacts

Observer log

What your team's lead declared, and what the room actually saw.

Commitment slip

Any deal you strike with another team. Logged. Shown to everyone — kept or broken.

Stakeholder map

The six people on a power / interest grid. Locked before Q1. Redrawn after Q4.

Expected every round. Not optional.

Hold each one up physically as you name it. "Expected every round. Not optional." The stakeholder map is the exception in timing — it locks once before Q1 and once after Q4 — but say the line as written; the pack explains the timing.
